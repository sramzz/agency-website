Straight answers

Before budget meets platform.

Which advertising platforms can you manage?

We can plan and manage campaigns across Google, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn and Pinterest, as well as ChatGPT Ads where the format and market are eligible. The recommended mix depends on your audience, objective, available creative and measurement setup.

Is advertising spend included in the management fee?

No. Campaign management and the advertising budget paid directly to each platform are separate. We agree the management scope, campaign count and proposed media budget before work begins.

What counts as one campaign?

One campaign covers one platform, objective, market and audience structure. Different platforms, countries or objectives may require separate campaigns so budgets, messages and results can be managed clearly.

Do you create the ads and landing pages?

Campaign planning includes message, format and conversion-path recommendations. Creative production and landing-page work are scoped according to what already exists, what each platform requires and what the campaign needs to convert effectively.

Can you guarantee a specific return on ad spend?

No credible agency can guarantee a fixed return. Performance depends on the offer, market, budget, competition, creative, website and sales follow-up. We establish measurement, test methodically and report what the evidence supports.

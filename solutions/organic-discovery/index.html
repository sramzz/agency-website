How discovery works

Search engines do not see your business the way customers do.

Before a platform can show, cite or recommend you, it needs enough accessible evidence to discover what you offer, connect it to an intent and trust it as a useful option.

01 / Discover

Can the system find the right information?

Clean technical foundations, crawlable pages, business profiles and consistent external references make your offer available to search systems.

02 / Understand

Can it connect your business to the intent?

Clear services, locations, entities, structured data and direct answers help machines understand when your business is relevant.

03 / Select

Is there enough evidence to surface you?

Useful content, authority, reviews, citations and real-world consistency increase the reasons to rank, cite or recommend your business.

Google usually returns ranked results. AI assistants synthesise an answer from available sources. The interfaces differ; the need for clear, credible evidence does not.

Straight answers

What businesses ask before starting.

What is the difference between SEO and GEO?

SEO helps search engines crawl, understand and rank your pages in search results. GEO helps generative systems understand your business well enough to cite or recommend it in AI answers. They overlap: strong technical foundations, useful content, clear entities and credible external signals support both.

Can you guarantee a first-place ranking or a ChatGPT recommendation?

No credible agency can control a search engine or AI model. We improve the signals these systems use, measure visibility and strengthen the path from discovery to contact. Rankings, citations and recommendations still depend on the market, competitors, platform changes and the evidence available about your business.

How do you measure organic discovery?

We establish a baseline, then track the signals that fit the project: qualified queries, impressions, rankings, website visits, map interactions, calls, direction requests, messages, bookings and recorded AI citations. The purpose is not more traffic at any cost; it is more relevant discovery that can become business.

How long does SEO and GEO take?

Technical fixes can be completed quickly, but search visibility compounds over time. The pace depends on your starting point, market, website and available evidence. We begin with the clearest gaps and report what changes, rather than promising a fixed ranking by an arbitrary date.

Do we need a new website?

Not always. We first assess whether the current website can support useful pages, technical improvements, measurement and clear conversion paths. If it can, we improve it. If the structure blocks discovery or contact, we explain the trade-offs before recommending a rebuild.

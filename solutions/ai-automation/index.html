The method

Document the work before automating it.

  1. 01

    Find the bottleneck

    Identify repetitive tasks, delayed handovers, missed follow-ups and manual work that consumes time or loses revenue.

  2. 02

    Map the work

    Document the trigger, people, tools, data, exceptions and successful outcome.

  3. 03

    Write the playbook

    Define business rules, required inputs, approvals and safeguards before anything is automated.

  4. 04

    Connect predictable steps

    Make records, files, reports, notifications and calendar updates happen reliably.

  5. 05

    Add AI deliberately

    Use AI for conversations, document creation, extraction or classification where fixed rules are not enough.

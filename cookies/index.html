Website storage

Cookies & Similar Storage

Last updated: 27 September 2026

This page explains how cookies and browser storage are used when you visit rankingrebels.com or submit an enquiry.

Useful details

A clear view of the storage used by this website.

Analytics

Measuring visits.

Google Analytics measures visits and how people use this website. Its GA4 tag normally uses the first-party cookies _ga and _ga_<container-id> to distinguish visitors and maintain session information. Google lists a default expiry of two years, although the actual duration can vary with our configuration and your browser. Google Analytics currently loads when you open this website. See Google's cookie information.

Form storage

Remembering a submission.

After a successful form submission, this site stores a submission identifier and time in your browser's localStorage under rr.lead.receipt.v1. The record is treated as valid for up to 30 days and is removed when the site next reads it after expiry. We also use sessionStorage under rr.lead.session.v1 to manage confirmation during your browser session. These records do not contain your form answers.

Security

Protecting the site and forms.

Cloudflare Turnstile checks form submissions for automated abuse. Depending on the security features active for a visit, Cloudflare may set necessary security cookies such as __cf_bm or cf_clearance. These cookies support bot detection or security challenges; they are not set for every visitor in every configuration. See Cloudflare's cookie information.

Your browser

Managing storage.

You can inspect or clear cookies and site storage in your browser settings. Clearing form storage may cause the enquiry form to appear again. We do not currently provide an on-site Analytics choice; this page will be updated when that control is available. For information about personal data, see our Privacy Policy or email info@rankingrebels.com.

Frequently asked questions.

How long do SEO and GEO take?

Timing depends on competition, the site’s starting point, authority and how quickly changes can be approved. We prioritise the work most likely to create measurable progress first.

Should paid ads run while organic visibility grows?

Often, yes. Paid campaigns can capture and test demand while organic work builds durable visibility. The right mix depends on urgency, economics and available evidence.

Do you guarantee rankings or AI citations?

No responsible agency can guarantee rankings, map positions or inclusion in generated answers. We improve the technical, content, entity and authority signals that influence visibility.

What happens in a search audit?

We review commercial demand, current visibility, content structure, local signals, paid activity, tracking and the path from landing page to enquiry.

What types of businesses do you work with?

Primarily service businesses that depend on high-intent searches to generate calls, messages, bookings, quotes or sales.

Can you work across different countries and languages?

Yes. Ranking Rebels actively supports Australia, the Netherlands and Latin America, adapting strategy to local language, competition and buying behaviour.

How do you measure success?

We connect visibility and campaign performance to meaningful actions such as qualified calls, forms, WhatsApp conversations, bookings and sales—not traffic alone.

Can we start with only one service?

Yes. You can begin with the most important constraint and add paid, organic or automation support when the evidence justifies it.

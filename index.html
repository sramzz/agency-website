Confidence check

Questions clients ask before we start.

How long do SEO and GEO take?

It depends on your market, your website and how quickly changes go live. We start with the work most likely to show measurable progress.

Should paid ads run while organic visibility grows?

Often, yes. Paid ads can bring in and test demand while organic visibility grows. We recommend the mix that suits your urgency, budget and available evidence.

Do you guarantee rankings or AI citations?

No. Google and AI platforms decide rankings, map positions and citations. We improve the content, technical setup and trust signals that give you a stronger chance of appearing.

What happens in a search audit?

We look at what customers search for, where you appear today, what competitors are doing, how tracking works and what happens between a visit and an enquiry.

What types of businesses do you work with?

Mostly service businesses that rely on calls, messages, bookings, quotes or sales from people who are actively looking for help.

Can you work across different countries and languages?

Yes. We work across Australia, the Netherlands and Latin America, adapting the language, channels and strategy to each market.

How do you measure success?

We look beyond traffic. Success is measured through qualified calls, forms, WhatsApp conversations, bookings and sales, using the measures that matter to your business.

Can we start with only one service?

Yes. We can start with the biggest obstacle today, then add organic, paid or automation support when it makes sense.

Frequently asked questions

What to know before hiring an SEO agency.

How long does local SEO take?

Local SEO is a long-term growth channel rather than an instant result. Timing depends on competition, the website's current condition, local authority, content quality, and how quickly recommended changes can be implemented.

Do Google Ads replace SEO?

No. Google Ads can capture existing demand quickly, while SEO builds organic visibility over time. Using both can provide faster market data and a more durable acquisition channel.

Are rankings guaranteed?

No ethical agency can guarantee a specific position because search results change by location, competition, device, and algorithm. Ranking Rebels improves the technical, content, authority, and conversion factors a business can control.

What types of businesses does Ranking Rebels work with?

Ranking Rebels works primarily with service businesses that depend on local or high-intent searches to generate calls, WhatsApp conversations, bookings, quotes, or sales.

Can Ranking Rebels work with businesses outside Australia?

Yes. Ranking Rebels supports businesses across Australia and selected European markets, with English and Spanish communication available.

Frequently asked questions

Before you start.

Does Ranking Rebels work with businesses across Medellín?

Yes. Strategy can target Medellín, surrounding municipalities and wider Colombian demand when the service area and commercial priorities support it.

Can SEO and paid campaigns run together?

Yes. Paid campaigns can test and capture demand while SEO, local search and GEO build durable organic visibility.

Do you guarantee rankings or AI citations?

No. No agency can guarantee rankings, map positions or citations in AI answers. We improve the technical, content, entity and local signals that influence visibility.

How is performance measured?

Measurement focuses on qualified calls, messages, forms, bookings and sales alongside the search and campaign data that explain those outcomes.

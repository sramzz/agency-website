Frequently asked questions

What to know before we work together.

What types of businesses do you work with?

We work with businesses that rely on calls, quote requests and bookings. That includes gyms, tradies, physio practices, beauty businesses and cosmetic surgery clinics. We shape the work around what you sell, the customers you want to reach and how they get in touch.

What does your digital marketing service include?

Depending on what your business needs, we can work on SEO, Google Business Profile, paid campaigns, landing pages, conversion tracking and follow-up automation. We start with the areas most likely to improve enquiries and sales.

What happens in a search audit?

We review where your business appears in search, what competitors are doing and what happens between a website visit and an enquiry. Then we identify priorities for your website, visibility, campaigns and tracking.

How long do SEO and GEO take?

It depends on the competition, your website and how quickly improvements go live. We agree on priorities and track progress as the work develops. Rankings and appearances in ChatGPT answers are decided by the platforms, so we cannot promise a fixed date or position.

Do your fees include advertising spend?

No. The plans cover the listed website and organic search work. Ads management is an add-on, charged at the campaign rates shown above. The budget paid directly to advertising platforms is separate from both.

Can you help us reach more than one service area?

Yes. We work with businesses across Australia, including regional centres and businesses with several locations. We plan your service and location pages around where you actually work, and report on performance by area.

What happens in the first month?

We sort out access and measurement, review your website and market, agree on priorities and start the first approved improvements. The audit helps us decide what to tackle first.

Can we start with one service?

Yes. We can start with organic visibility, paid ads or automation, depending on what your business needs now. We recommend additional work when it supports your goals and budget.

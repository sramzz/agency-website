Common questions

Before we work together.

How long does SEO take in Melbourne?

SEO timing depends on competition, the website's starting point, local authority and how quickly changes can be approved. An audit can identify immediate priorities, but durable visibility usually develops through consistent technical, content and authority work over time.

What is the difference between SEO and Local SEO?

SEO improves organic discovery across search engines and AI-led environments. Local SEO focuses on proximity and location signals such as Google Maps, Google Business Profile, reviews and locally relevant pages. Many Melbourne service businesses need both.

What is GEO or AI Search optimisation?

Generative Engine Optimisation makes a business easier for AI-led discovery systems to understand through clear entities, useful answers, structured data, credible sources and consistent facts. It complements SEO rather than replacing it.

Can Ranking Rebels improve my Google Maps visibility?

Ranking Rebels can improve the inputs that support local visibility, including Google Business Profile quality, website relevance, reviews, business information and measurement. Exact map positions cannot be guaranteed.

Do you work with businesses across all Melbourne suburbs?

Ranking Rebels can plan around the parts of Greater Melbourne a business genuinely serves. Individual suburb pages are recommended only when the business can support them with unique, useful local information.

Do I need Google Ads as well as SEO?

Not always. Paid search can capture and test demand sooner, while SEO builds durable visibility. The audit identifies whether organic, paid or a connected approach best fits the opportunity and budget.

Do you guarantee first-page rankings?

No. Search results, map positions and AI citations are controlled by third-party platforms. Ranking Rebels improves the technical, content, local, authority and conversion inputs that influence visibility and enquiries.

How much does SEO cost in Melbourne?

Investment depends on the market, starting point, number of priority services and the mix of SEO, Local SEO, paid acquisition and conversion work required. The search audit is used to define an appropriate scope.

What happens during a search audit?

The audit reviews search demand, technical foundations, service and location relevance, Google Business Profile, authority, competitors, conversion paths and measurement to identify where qualified demand is being lost.

Is paid media spend included in management fees?

No. Campaign management and the direct media budget paid to platforms such as Google or Meta are separate costs.

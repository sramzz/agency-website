Frequently asked questions

What to know before we work together.

Can you target Dutch and English searches?

Yes. We research Dutch and English demand separately because the same service can attract different audiences and buying intent in each language.

What does your digital marketing service include?

Depending on what your business needs, we can work on SEO, Google Business Profile, paid campaigns, landing pages, conversion tracking and follow-up automation.

What happens in a search audit?

We review where your business appears, what customers search for, what competitors are doing and what happens between a visit and an enquiry. Then we set the priorities.

How long do SEO and ChatGPT visibility take?

It depends on the competition, your website and how quickly improvements go live. Search engines and AI platforms decide visibility, so no fixed date or position can be promised.

Does your fee include advertising spend?

No. Campaign management and the budget paid directly to advertising platforms are separate. We agree the scope and budget before campaigns begin.

Can we start with one service?

Yes. We can start with organic visibility, paid ads or automation, depending on the biggest obstacle your business faces now.

Frequently asked questions

Before you invest.

Can a Netherlands strategy target Dutch and English searches?

Yes. Keyword research, landing architecture and campaign targeting should separate language and intent instead of assuming direct translations behave the same way.

Does local SEO matter for service businesses in the Netherlands?

Yes. Google Business Profile, consistent business information, relevant reviews and market-specific solution pages help nearby customers understand and contact the business.

Can paid search support the organic strategy?

Yes. Paid search can reveal valuable queries and capture immediate demand while organic search and GEO foundations develop.

Are rankings or AI citations guaranteed?

No. Rankings, map positions and AI citations cannot be guaranteed. We improve the signals that influence visibility and report outcomes in context.

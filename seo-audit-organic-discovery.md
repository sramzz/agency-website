## Critical fixes completed

1. Replaced the generic hero promise with a specific customer outcome: turning search intent into a customer.
2. Added a clear explanation of how discovery works across ranked results and generative answers.
3. Built the complete intent → discovery → contact → outcome narrative requested for Google and ChatGPT journeys.
4. Added documented proof from existing case studies without inventing testimonials or guarantees.
5. Consolidated conversion around one repeated action: `Audit my search visibility`.

## Important fixes completed

1. Added direct FAQ answers for SEO vs GEO, measurement, timing, guarantees and website requirements.
2. Added social metadata, a canonical URL and crawl-visible content.
3. Added a mobile CTA that appears only after the hero leaves the viewport.
4. Reserved stable 16:9-style visual areas for future motion or photography.
5. Included explicit caveats for platform control and past performance.

## Recommended follow-up after launch

1. Review Search Console queries after enough impressions accrue; update the primary phrase only from observed demand.
2. Add two to three inbound internal links from relevant market and case-study pages using descriptive anchors such as `SEO and GEO services`.
3. Replace or enhance the media frames with lightweight WebM/MP4 loops when approved; preserve their dimensions and reduced-motion fallback.
4. Validate the rendered schema and monitor whether FAQ rich-result eligibility changes; schema remains useful for machine comprehension even without a visual rich result.

Recommendation

Build wellness inside the existing Titanium website.

Use titanium-gym.com/wellness/. Wellness serves an overlapping audience at the same Airport West location, so keeping it together gives the new offer the best start.

Options assessment

The subdirectory wins on every practical criterion.

The four criteria cover how Titanium attracts demand, moves customers between services, structures the brand, and manages the website.

Comparison of the three website structures considered for Titanium wellness

Implementation brief

Keep the name broad and the next step simple.

The public-facing name can remain Titanium Recovery & Wellness. The shorter URL gives the offer room to expand.

Page definition

URL
titanium-gym.com/wellness/
Page title
Recovery & Wellness Airport West | Titanium Gym
H1
Premium Recovery and Wellness in Airport West

Why not /recovery-wellness/? Both URLs can rank, but /wellness/ is shorter, easier to communicate, cleaner in advertising, and broad enough for future services. Search relevance comes mainly from the title, headings, content, internal links, local signals, and individual service pages. Adding both words to the URL offers little additional benefit.

Domain protection

Buy the name. Do not build a second website.

If available, purchase titanium-wellness.com for signage, print, and campaigns, then use a permanent 301 redirect.

titanium-wellness.com 301 → titanium-gym.com/wellness/

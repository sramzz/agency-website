# Search investment decision card

An original planning worksheet for **SEO or Google Ads? How to Prioritise a Limited Marketing Budget**. It supports a decision about the next allocation, not an automatic budget split, forecast or investment guarantee.

## 4. Hypothetical sensitivity exercise

These inputs are invented for teaching. They are not a quote, Australian benchmark, forecast, campaign or Ranking Rebels results. Currency: AUD excluding GST throughout.

| Input | Supplied assumption |
|---|---:|
| Total included marketing allowance | 2,400 |
| Included campaign-specific setup/management/page/measurement | 800 |
| Media allocation | 1,600 |
| Assumed average CPC | 8 |
| Arithmetic clicks if media is spent at that average | 200 |
| Contribution per first job before marketing/other excluded costs | 500 |

The contribution assumption subtracts the stated variable delivery costs, including attributable labour, materials and subcontracting. It is not revenue, complete profit or cash received. Each modelled customer is first-time and has one first job. The customer counts below are alternative assumptions, not outcomes predicted by the 200 clicks.

| Hypothetical first-time customers/jobs | Contribution before marketing | Less included allowance | Planning balance before other costs |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 2 | 1,000 | 2,400 | −1,400 |
| 4 | 2,000 | 2,400 | −400 |
| 6 | 3,000 | 2,400 | 600 |

Under unchanged assumptions, 2,400 ÷ 500 = 4.8; **five first jobs** cover only the included marketing costs. This is not net-business break-even, a probability of obtaining five jobs or causal ROI. If contribution or costs change, recalculate.

Actual mixed campaigns need existing-customer work retained and costs/value scoped appropriately. Do not label every won job a first-time customer. Do not invent lifetime value or remove repeat work merely to make an acquisition calculation convenient.

For an SEO option, state the real task, effort, costs and evidence to observe. Do not manufacture a monthly traffic or enquiry curve just to fill a symmetrical table.

Search clicks, sessions, enquiries, jobs and people are not interchangeable. Keep unknown attribution and pending outcomes visible. If conversion counts are zero, do not report acquisition cost as zero. Before-and-after changes or attributed totals alone do not establish incremental impact.

Scene: two colleagues at an Australian service business are standing beside a simple work planning board in their ordinary office. One colleague holds a small work folder; the other points to one selected task card. The board contains five spacious, intentionally simplified task cards with large legible pictograms, including an enquiry envelope leading to a customer record, a brief summary document, a checklist, possible duplicate documents and a report. A restrained red outline singles out the enquiry-to-record card. The other tasks remain visible and unselected, suggesting a decision, not a claim of success or savings. Use a side-on narrative scene with the people engaged with the board, rather than another overhead view of hands on a cream desk. Medium: mature graphic editorial ink illustration with firm silhouettes, selective dry brush grain and clear simplified forms, consistent with the first two style references. Lighting and palette: a predominantly charcoal and black scene with generous readable off-white highlights on the people and task cards; distinctive Ranking Rebels red, no other saturated colours. It should feel focused and practical, never gloomy or threatening. Wide landscape composition, generous breathing room, keep faces, hands and task board well inside the image edges. No readable words, headings, numbers, software brands, fake testimonials, money, metrics, robots, gears, neon, gradients, photorealistic stock-photo look, sepia wash or giant paper background.

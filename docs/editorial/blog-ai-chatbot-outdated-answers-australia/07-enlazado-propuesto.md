Condición: ambos destinos públicos y versión aprobada; coordinar con otras propuestas para no duplicar párrafos. Fuente y HTML juntos, fechas originales conservadas.

### 16 → 19

Lugar: Make the next failure easier to notice and resolve.

Después de:

Reliable connections can reduce repeated data entry, make handovers easier to verify and help the team recover work consistently. The benefit comes from the controls and working habits, and should be assessed against their setup and maintenance effort. Adding AI is optional; it does not by itself repair access, mappings or recovery behaviour.

Añadir:

If the connection works but a chatbot still gives an old answer, trace [the source and evidence behind that reply](https://rankingrebels.com/blog/ai-chatbot-outdated-answers/). An up-to-date record and the material selected for an answer are separate checks.

Condición: ambos destinos públicos y versión aprobada; coordinar con otras propuestas para no duplicar párrafos. Fuente y HTML juntos, fechas originales conservadas.

### 19 → 16

Lugar: Six places an answer can lose touch with the business → The connection has not brought the change through.

Después de:

Ask for evidence that the relevant new content is available to the answering system. A recent job timestamp alone is insufficient: it might refer to another source, an incomplete job or a run with warnings. Microsoft's [indexer guidance](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/search/search-how-to-create-indexers) explicitly distinguishes monitoring execution from checking indexed content.

Añadir:

If the evidence points to the connection itself, our guide to [business apps that have stopped syncing](https://rankingrebels.com/blog/business-apps-stopped-syncing/) follows a source record through the affected route before attempting recovery.

Condición: ambos destinos públicos y versión aprobada; coordinar con otras propuestas para no duplicar párrafos. Fuente y HTML juntos, fechas originales conservadas.

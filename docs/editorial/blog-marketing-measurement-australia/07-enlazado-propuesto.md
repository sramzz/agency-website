## Entradas exactas preparadas

### SEO and AI search

Después de:

People may encounter your business without clicking, return directly or use several channels before contacting you. Record what you can connect to an enquiry and where information is missing. Consider seasonality, advertising, platform changes and competitors before attributing a change to a page edit.

Añadir:

To connect those records with costs and a decision, our guide to [measuring digital marketing results](https://rankingrebels.com/blog/measure-digital-marketing-results/) explains how to compare enquiry groups and keep attributed revenue separate from profit.

### Local SEO and Google Maps

Después de:

Review campaigns, seasonal demand and operational changes alongside visibility. An increase after an edit is not automatically caused by that edit.

Añadir:

When you review the business result, use [defined enquiry groups and costs](https://rankingrebels.com/blog/measure-digital-marketing-results/) so profile activity, won work and revenue do not become interchangeable.

### What Is SEO?

Después de:

Include writing, development, specialist and maintenance costs when assessing the investment. State the attribution method and its limitations. An average ranking or total traffic figure cannot tell the whole story, and a dashboard alone does not prove the extra revenue caused by each change.

Añadir:

For a fuller review of enquiry quality, customer outcomes and costs, our guide to [measuring digital marketing results](https://rankingrebels.com/blog/measure-digital-marketing-results/) explains what the records can establish and where uncertainty remains.

### Website Visits but Few Enquiries?

Después de:

Record what changed and compare relevant audiences over a meaningful period. With few enquiries, a small numerical change can produce a large percentage swing. Different traffic sources, seasonality and simultaneous campaigns can also change the mix. A before-and-after improvement is evidence to examine, rather than proof that one edit caused it.

Añadir:

Once enquiries are reaching the team, our guide to [evaluating marketing results from visits to customers](https://rankingrebels.com/blog/measure-digital-marketing-results/) helps you follow their outcomes and assess the costs on a consistent basis.

### How Google Ads Works for Service Businesses

Después de:

In practice, records may be incomplete and sales can take time. Keep recent enquiries with unresolved outcomes separate rather than treating them as lost or combining them with customers from older campaigns. Label an unresolved group as provisional and record the reporting cut-off and follow-up period. If a denominator is zero, report that measure as unavailable.

Añadir:

To extend that review to completed work and the business’s costs, our guide to [measuring marketing results](https://rankingrebels.com/blog/measure-digital-marketing-results/) separates attributed revenue, contribution and incremental impact.

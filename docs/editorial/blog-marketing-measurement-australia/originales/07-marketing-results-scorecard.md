# Marketing results scorecard for a service business

An original educational worksheet prepared for Ranking Rebels on 21 October 2026. All completed examples are fictional. They are not benchmarks, campaign observations or results achieved by Ranking Rebels. No account or customer data was used.

Use it to prepare a decision, rather than to collect every available metric. Start with a business outcome, define the evidence and record what remains uncertain.

An awareness or educational objective can have useful intermediate indicators. State what those indicators demonstrate and what further evidence would be needed before claiming customer or revenue impact.

## 2. Keep the stages and statuses separate

| Record | Definition to agree | Evidence source |
|---|---|---|
| Contact interaction | Precisely which measured action occurred | Analytics or relevant platform |
| Received enquiry | A retrievable request reached the business | Authorised receiving/call records |
| Qualified enquiry | Agreed service, location and fit criteria met | Assessment record and reason |
| Won enquiry / accepted job | A qualified enquiry resulted in an accepted job under the agreed sales process | Opportunity or job record |
| New customer | Agreed first-customer definition met | Sales record |
| Revenue | State whether booked, recognised, invoiced or collected, and adjustments | Financial records on the chosen basis |

Deduplicate repeated contact about the same opportunity under an agreed rule. Keep qualification separate from sales outcome: a qualified enquiry can be lost. Keep pending assessment and unresolved sales visible, with counts and dates. Record why an enquiry was disqualified, without treating an unanswered assessment as proof of poor quality.

Keep won enquiries/jobs separate from first-time customers. An existing customer may accept another job, and one customer may have several opportunities. Identify distinct new customers against the business's customer records; do not count every accepted quote as a new customer. For a win rate, each qualified enquiry contributes at most one won outcome even if it produces several jobs.

If a contact cannot be reliably associated with an advertising interaction or session, report the available counts separately. Do not fabricate a complete funnel. Keep customer identifiers and private notes in authorised systems; this public worksheet should use anonymous or aggregate data.

## 3. Reconcile reports before combining them

For each figure, note its platform, metric, report and scope. Search Console clicks, Analytics sessions and recorded enquiries are different units. Check source definitions, date basis, time zone, attribution model/window, processing status and tracking coverage when comparing.

Do not add attributed conversions from separate platforms and call the result unique customers. Do not divide unique people or enquiries by sessions and label it a session conversion rate. A session-based rate requires a matching session-based numerator and denominator.

For a qualification rate, use qualified enquiries divided by unique enquiries in the defined group, while reporting how many remain unassessed. For a qualified-enquiry win rate, use qualified enquiries that resulted in an accepted job divided by qualified enquiries in the same group, with unresolved outcomes disclosed. Keep new-customer counts separate for acquisition calculations. If a campaign serves repeat customers too, state the scope and cost allocation. Use zero denominators as unavailable, not as a calculated zero rate.

## 4. Worked example with a defined cost basis

This is a hypothetical acquired group, not a calendar month's new enquiries mixed with older customers. At the observation cut-off, all 30 unique enquiries have been assessed: 18 are disqualified and 12 qualified. Three of the qualified enquiries each produced one accepted job and the other nine were lost. The three jobs belong to three distinct first-time customers. No assessment or sales outcome remains pending. The work has been completed; revenue and the stated delivery costs are recorded for that work.

All amounts are AUD, consistently excluding GST. The example assumes the stated association with one paid campaign is supported by the records; it does not demonstrate incremental impact.

| Measure | Calculation or input | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Media spend | Given | 1,500 |
| Allocated campaign management and page work | Given | 600 |
| Included marketing cost | 1,500 + 600 | 2,100 |
| Unique received enquiries | Given | 30 |
| Qualified enquiries | Given | 12 |
| Qualified enquiries won / accepted jobs in this example | Given; one job per won enquiry | 3 |
| Distinct first-time customers from those jobs | Given; one customer per job in this example | 3 |
| Qualification rate | 12 ÷ 30 | 40% |
| Qualified-enquiry win rate | 3 won enquiries ÷ 12 qualified enquiries | 25% |
| Media cost per received enquiry | 1,500 ÷ 30 | 50 |
| Included marketing cost per new customer | 2,100 ÷ 3 | 700 |
| Recorded revenue from completed work | Given | 7,500 |
| Stated direct delivery costs | Given | 3,600 |
| Contribution before marketing and other overheads | 7,500 − 3,600 | 3,900 |
| Contribution after included marketing costs, before other costs | 3,900 − 2,100 | 1,800 |
| Revenue ROAS using media spend | 7,500 ÷ 1,500 | 5× |

The AUD 700 includes only the stated marketing costs. It is not complete company-wide customer acquisition cost. The AUD 1,800 is not net business profit or a complete ROI calculation. The 5× is attributed revenue per dollar of media spend, not profit per dollar. No forecast or recommendation to increase spending follows from this illustration.

## 5. Record a decision with its limits

| Observation or question | Evidence needed | Possible action | Owner / next check |
|---|---|---|---|
| Contact events rose, received enquiries did not | Event definitions and authorised delivery checks | Investigate measurement/delivery before assuming stronger demand | |
| Enquiries rose, but many remain unassessed | Assessment coverage and responsibility | Complete assessments; label quality estimate provisional | |
| Attributed customers increased | Comparable cohort, costs, capacity and alternative explanations | Review economics and evidence before scaling | |
| Platform totals exceed unique sales records | Definitions, windows, duplicate credit and missing records | Reconcile; retain an explicit unexplained difference if unresolved | |

These are investigation examples, not observations from a real business. A before-and-after pattern does not isolate causality. Where the decision requires incremental impact, consider an appropriately designed experiment with qualified support and sufficient data; availability of a platform tool is not assured.

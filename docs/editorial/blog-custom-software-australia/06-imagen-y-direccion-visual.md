## Prompt utilizado

Create one original premium editorial illustration for Ranking Rebels, an Australian digital agency blog. Topic: custom business software can fill one specific gap while existing business systems keep doing their jobs. Landscape 3:2 composition, designed to crop slightly to a wide blog cover. Scene: a commercial maintenance business owner in a plain work shirt stands at a simple office counter beside a workshop, thoughtfully reviewing a modest laptop and a neatly opened project folder. On the laptop, a deliberately simplified customer document portal appears as three large document silhouettes and one small approval mark, with no readable text or numbers. A larger, established job-record ledger rests beside it. A single restrained dark red line visually connects the ledger to the small portal, suggesting a carefully scoped connection, not a wholesale system replacement. Make the person and physical documents the subject, not a software dashboard. Mature editorial ink drawing with dry brush texture, fine crosshatching, soft paper grain and broad quiet negative space. Palette strictly warm off-white paper, charcoal, graphite greys, with only 5 percent muted oxblood red accents. Light paper dominates; avoid both pitch-black overall darkness and candy colour. Strong clear silhouettes, sophisticated print-magazine composition, flat perspective, understated warmth. No blue, green, orange, purple, neon, glowing effects, robots, gears, puzzle pieces, coins, stock photography, 3D plastic, gradients, fake brand logos, charts, marketing badges, text or labels. Standalone finished image, no decorative frame.

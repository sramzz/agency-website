# Visibility diagnosis: local editorial preview

Scope: isolated content-site preparation. Reuse the established public blog shell, Oswald/Inter/JetBrains Mono typography, reading navigation, responsive table behaviour and progress script. The approved editorial criteria and runtime take precedence over older unrelated design directions in the repository.

The scoped reading tokens and breakpoints are inherited from the blog 5 local experiment. `preview.css` owns the light/dark aliases for this article only. Light uses off-white paper #f7f6f2, charcoal prose #252a30, headings #12161c, secondary text #53606c and dark red links #b51c38. Dark maps to #050609, #c7cbd3, #f8fafc and #94a3b8. The public shared styles remain the owner of brand typography, cover and layout.

Both variants use one Markdown manuscript, renderer, illustration and diagram. They are proposals for review, not an approved global template. The user considered an earlier colourful illustration too abrupt. This new hero therefore uses near-monochrome ink and paper with restrained red accents, an overhead desk composition and no invented account data. Its subject is checking a page, distinct from the previous landscaper portrait.

The four-path SVG is a deterministic editorial aid with desktop and mobile formats. It does not imitate Search Console, present observed statistics or imply a guaranteed progression to ranking. Paths can coexist. Prose and alt provide the essential meaning.

Keep semantic links, table headers, visible focus, native mobile disclosure, reserved media dimensions and reduced-motion support. No new motion, forms, account states or customer information are needed. Static/resource checks and asset inspection are recorded separately from full-page browser QA. Selection and publication remain outside this preparation.

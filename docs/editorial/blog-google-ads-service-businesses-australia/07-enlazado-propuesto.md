## Entradas exactas preparadas

### What Is SEO?

Después de:

Neither is automatically the better choice. You might use advertising to test an offer while improving important website pages. A short campaign may need a different plan from an ongoing service. A confusing offer or a broken form can waste visits from either source.

Añadir:

For the paid-search side, our guide to [how Google Ads works for service businesses](https://rankingrebels.com/blog/google-ads-for-service-businesses/) follows a search through the ad, contact route and enquiry, with the costs and outcomes kept separate.

### Website Visits but Few Enquiries?

Después de:

Look beyond the total visit count. Compare service pages with information pages, paid with organic visits, and relevant locations with places outside your scope. Our guide to [what SEO involves](https://rankingrebels.com/blog/what-is-seo/) explains why a service search and a general advice question need different pages.

Añadir:

If those visits come from Google Search ads, check the query and ad promise alongside the page. Our guide to [Google Ads for service businesses](https://rankingrebels.com/blog/google-ads-for-service-businesses/) explains that route and how to assess the enquiries it produces.

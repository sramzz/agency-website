# TikTok fit and test brief for a service business

An original educational resource prepared for Ranking Rebels on 23 October 2026. This is a planning proposal, not an account setup, a published asset, a campaign result or a validated scoring model.

| Question | Record the business's answer and evidence |

This brief does not require certainty about every audience assumption. Distinguish an uncertainty a test can investigate from an unsupported claim, missing permission or failed contact route that needs repair first.

## 3. Fictional example: a Perth landscaping business

The business serves selected suburbs and wants enquiries for its defined project work. Its presence on TikTok, audience suitability and campaign performance have not been established. No real business, footage, advert or result is represented.

| Proposed concept | Question answered | What would be needed before use |
|---|---|---|
| Prepare for a quote | What information should I bring? | Accurate service location/context example and a truthful request |
| Understand the scope | Do you handle this kind of project? | Real inclusions/exclusions; any project example supported and authorised |
| Know the next step | What happens after I enquire? | A response and assessment process the business can actually deliver |

These are three possible concepts, not a minimum number, performance prediction or A/B experiment. No concept has been produced, approved or tested. A comparison would need a stated question and evaluation design; uneven delivery alone does not isolate a creative's causal effect.

## 4. Verify the contact route

Choose an available instant form, website route or conversation product on its merits. Record:

- What information is necessary and what can wait.
- What the person is told will happen next.
- How a successful enquiry reaches a retrievable record.
- Who receives it, assesses it and takes the next action.
- How repeated messages, wrong-area enquiries, unknowns and opt-outs are handled.
- What event is reported and what it actually represents.

A tap, conversation, received enquiry, qualified enquiry, opportunity, accepted job and first-time customer are different units. Define identity and project matching before counting. Keep pending assessments visible. Use authorised synthetic tests where appropriate and identify test records so they do not become sales activity.

Check account currency and budget mode. A platform minimum is not evidence that the test can answer the business question. A daily figure may behave differently under different modes; do not assume an identical hard cap for every day. No universal AUD budget or duration is set by this worksheet.

## 6. Record observations without inventing a funnel

| Observation | Definition / source / period | Actual result |
|---|---|---|
| Exposure or video engagement | Exact platform metric and eligible content | |
| Contact interaction | Tap, form or conversation metric as actually defined | |
| Unique enquiries received | Retrievable cases under a stated identity/project rule | |
| Qualified / unsuitable / unassessed | Same relevant group and assessment rules | |
| Accepted work | Evidence and business definition | |
| Distinct first-time customers | Checked against existing customers | |
| Included cost | Media or stated fuller scope, with consistent currency/tax basis | |
| Attributed outcomes | Objective, route, windows and metric definition | |
| Limits and next decision | Unknowns, incomplete links, changes and other explanations | |

Every result cell is intentionally blank. This worksheet has no observed performance. Do not call zero outcomes a zero cost per result; keep an undefined ratio unavailable. Do not divide events by unrelated sessions or customers from a different period.

If comparing cost per received enquiry and cost per qualified enquiry, retain the same relevant group, period and cost basis, showing unassessed cases. Neither figure is automatically complete acquisition cost, profit or additional revenue caused by advertising.

## 7. Choose an action with evidence

- **Repair:** a contact route fails, an important claim lacks support or required permission is missing.
- **Investigate through a bounded test:** the audience/message question is plausible and the planned observation can inform a decision.
- **Change the next priority:** the necessary creative, audience evidence or response capacity is not yet practical.

These are proposed decision categories, not a score that certifies channel suitability. Record why the evidence supports the action, who owns it and what remains uncertain. Ordinary platform attribution is different from a study of additional outcomes caused by ads.

# AI automation landing page: section decision

## Page summary

- URL: https://rankingrebels.com/solutions/ai-automation/
- Primary query inferred from the page: `AI automation for service businesses`
- Role: evergreen commercial service page
- Decision: remove the “Adding AI to a leaky process only moves the leak” section.

## Why removal is the stronger option

The idea is sound, but the section repeats information already communicated more clearly elsewhere:

- The hero visual already explains a mapped enquiry workflow with rules, AI assistance and human approval.
- The next section explains the five-step method, beginning with finding the bottleneck and mapping the work.
- The human-handoff section separately explains where rules, AI and people belong.

The removed section added no new service, use case, proof, objection answer or commercially relevant detail. Its headline was memorable but did not strengthen the page's target topic enough to justify a full additional scroll.

Removing this block should not create a meaningful SEO loss. The page retains its keyword-focused title, H1, introductory copy, service examples, method, FAQ content and Service/FAQ structured data. The useful internal link to the delivery journey now sits in the method section, where it has clearer contextual relevance.

## Competitive finding

Pages competing for AI automation service searches tend to add value with concrete service categories, workflow examples, integrations, project scope, pricing, compliance or evidence. Examples reviewed:

- AI Automation Company Australia: https://aiautomationcompany.com.au/services
- Aivy: https://aivy.com.au/
- Hello People: https://hellopeople.com.au/ai-solutions/ai-automation-and-workflows
- Zatersio: https://zatersio.com.au/automation

Ranking Rebels already has strong workflow examples and a clear method. The most useful future addition would be verified proof: a real before/after case study with the initial workflow, implementation and measured result. A generic substitute should not be added until that evidence exists.

## On-page impact

| Dimension | Result after removal |
| --- | --- |
| Title and meta | Pass; unchanged and aligned with the target service |
| Header structure | Pass; one H1 and a logical sequence of descriptive H2s |
| Body content | Improved; less repetition and faster progression from value to method |
| Internal links | Preserved; the journey link was moved into the method section |
| Images/media | Improved; one synthetic placeholder visual was removed |
| Schema | Pass; visible FAQ content and JSON-LD remain aligned |

## Recommended future replacement

Add a compact, real case study only when verified evidence is available. It should state the original bottleneck, connected systems, human approval point and measured operational or revenue result. Do not replace the removed section with another conceptual diagram.

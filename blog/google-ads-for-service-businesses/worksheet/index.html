The search terms report provides only part of the query picture. Record which evidence is available, rather than claiming every search is visible. Review available terms together with enquiry outcomes.

3. Keep business events distinct

EventEvidence requiredOwner
Contact interactionMeasured click or other interaction, labelled accurately
Received enquiryMessage or connected contact recorded by the business
Qualified enquiryAgreed criteria assessed, with reason/status
New customerWon-customer definition met and recorded

Use agreed statuses such as received, awaiting assessment, qualified, disqualified, quote pending and won or lost. Record a reason for disqualification, for example an unoffered service or an address outside coverage. Keep outcome status separate from qualification: a qualified enquiry may still be lost, and an unresolved enquiry is not automatically disqualified. Adapt these labels to the business rather than assuming they fit every sales process.

Decide how repeat contact about one job is deduplicated. Avoid duplicate tracking imports for the same outcome. Distinguish unresolved enquiries from disqualified enquiries. Do not place names, emails, phone numbers or sensitive customer details in a public copy of this worksheet.

Review the campaign's actual goals and conversion actions, not only the label “primary” or “secondary”. A secondary action in a custom goal can still be used for bidding. Record tracking coverage, known gaps and how later outcomes are associated with the original advertising interaction.

4. Compare costs using the same group of enquiries

This is the same hypothetical example as the article. All amounts are AUD, excluding GST on a consistent basis. It describes a tracked group whose sales outcomes have had sufficient time to be assessed, not a calendar month's recent leads mixed with older sales.

Do not calculate a rate with a zero denominator; report it as unavailable and state the underlying counts. If outcomes are still unresolved, identify the group as provisional. Specify your observation cut-off, follow-up period, attribution approach and missing data.

These measures are not profit, complete company-wide acquisition cost or proof of incremental sales. To assess economics, account for revenue, service delivery costs and other relevant costs. Do not treat a configured lead value as realised revenue.

5. Turn observations into an investigation

Record the observation, possible explanation, evidence needed, owner and next review. For example:

ObservationInvestigationEvidence that would change the decision
Form clicks rise, but received messages do notTest delivery and event definitionsConfirmed delivery records or a reproducible failure
Many enquiries request an unoffered serviceReview queries, ad promise and page scopeA consistent pattern of mismatched requests
Suitable enquiries are not quotedReview responsibility, capacity and follow-upRecorded reasons and response history

Changing a budget is not a substitute for identifying the problem. A small sample supports a tentative investigation, not a definitive diagnosis.
